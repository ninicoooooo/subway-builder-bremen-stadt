## Population and demand provenance

Population label: Statistische Ämter des Bundes und der Länder, Zensus 2022, reference date 15 May 2022; 100 m population grid clipped by cell midpoint to [8.45, 52.93, 9.10, 53.30]. Grid sum 965,312. This population label does not equal the number of represented commuters.
https://www.zensus2022.de/

Demand inputs: © Statistische Ämter des Bundes und der Länder, Deutschland, 2026, Regionaldatenbank Deutschland, table 19321-Z-22, Pendlerrechnung der Länder, reference date 30 June 2024. License: Datenlizenz Deutschland – Namensnennung – Version 2.0 (dl-de/by-2-0), as stated in the saved source-table response. Data modified for synthetic point-level commuter allocation.
https://www.regionalstatistik.de/
https://www.govdata.de/dl-de/by-2-0

Employment calibration: © Statistik der Bundesagentur für Arbeit, municipality employment statistics (2022). Data modified into model weights. Redistribution with source attribution follows the provider's usage conditions.
https://statistik.arbeitsagentur.de/
